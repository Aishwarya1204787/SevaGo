About SevaGo

SevaGo is a hyperlocal delivery service built for the daily routine of Vadodara.

Too many phone numbers

One number for the tiffin wala, another for the medical store, another for a courier. Nobody remembers them all when they are in a hurry.

Unclear prices

Local errand runners often quote a different price each time, so customers cannot plan or compare.

No one to send

Working couples, students and elderly parents often have nobody free to run a small errand across the city.

What SevaGo does

We pick up from a home kitchen, a medical store, a kirana shop, an office or a house, and deliver to the address you choose. Price depends on the service and the distance between two Vadodara areas, and you can see it before you book.

Our value to you

One booking number, a price you know before you book, delivery in under an hour for most trips, and riders whose licences we verify.

Who we serve

Vadodara is a city of students, offices, factories and close-knit families, and each group has its own daily errand.

  • Students and young professionals. Around The Maharaja Sayajirao University (MSU), Fatehgunj and Sayajigunj, who order tiffin, groceries and documents to hostels and PGs.
  • Offices and working professionals. In business areas like Alkapuri, Akota, Subhanpura and Gotri, who need lunch, files and parcels sent in under an hour.
  • Industrial belts and small businesses. Around Makarpura and Waghodia Road, where offices and shops need document runs and supplies without sending staff out.
  • Families and elderly residents. In Manjalpur, Tandalja, Vasna and Gorwa, who rely on medicines and groceries brought to the door.
  • Home cooks, chemists and kirana shops. Local sellers who gain delivery to customers beyond their own lane without owning a rider.
  • Festival season. During Navratri, when Vadodara is busiest, we handle extra tiffin, laundry and gift orders.

Trust and safety

Partner home kitchens must hold FSSAI registration (rules and registration are on the FSSAI FoSCoS portal). Riders must hold a valid driving licence (licence and vehicle rules are on the Parivahan Sewa portal).
